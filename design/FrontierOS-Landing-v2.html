A research-context operating system

Eight engines that watch the frontier for your work.

01◎

Near My Work Score

Scores every new paper by how close it is to your draft, notes, and datasets.

Compares public research against your private memory — and explains the overlap.
02▦

Private Research Memory

Upload drafts, notes, proposals, datasets and papers into a graph only you can see.

A private graph of your direction, compared against the global frontier.
03⟁

Research Directives

Create a directive once; FrontierOS tracks the field continuously — no manual search.

“Alert me if someone publishes work overlapping my novelty.”
04⛨

Novelty Risk Radar

Detects papers that overlap your claimed contribution and suggests how to reposition.

“Three new papers overlap your method. Your novelty is strongest in evaluation design.”
05⇄

Living Related Work

Suggests new citations, tells where they fit, and writes the sentence as the field moves.

Never let your related work go stale — your citation map updates itself.
06❝

Citation Advisor

Ask “should I cite this?” and get a verdict, where to cite, why, and the sentence.

Yes / No · where · why · suggested citation · backed by evidence.
07⚑

Threat or Opportunity

Classifies each nearby paper: must cite, competing, baseline, dataset, collaborator.

“Threat: Medium. Overlaps your benchmark, but your reliability angle holds.”
08⌥

Paper-to-Project Builder

Turns a paper into a reproduction plan, code skeleton, baselines, and a roadmap.

From paper discovery to a one-week project roadmap.
